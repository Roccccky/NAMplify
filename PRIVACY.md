# NAMplify – privacy

NAMplify includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting telemetry.

Your library, presets, settings and recordings stay on your computer. When you sign in to TONE3000,
NAMplify stores the session it gets back in its settings file on your computer, so you stay signed in.
Signing out removes it.

NAMplify only connects to TONE3000 (`tone3000.com` and the hosts it serves tones and pictures from),
and only for what you do in the browser: searching, downloading and opening a tone's page.
